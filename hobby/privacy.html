Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy applies to the Inbox Zero instance at inbox.tasker.me (the "app"). Richard Tasker operates the app for his own personal use. It is a self-hosted copy of the open-source Inbox Zero project. It is not a public service, and only the operator's own accounts can sign in.

Data the app accesses

When you connect a Google account, the app can access:

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Gmail messages, labels, and settings, so that the app can sort, label, archive, and draft replies to email.
  • Google Calendar events, if you connect a calendar, to prepare meeting briefings.
  • Google Drive files that the app creates or that you select, if you connect Drive, to file attachments.

How the app uses the data

The app uses this data only to provide its email assistant features to the account owner. The app does not sell data, does not use data for advertising, and does not share data with other people.

Service providers

The app uses these providers to run:

  • Vercel: hosting.
  • Neon: database storage.
  • Upstash: temporary data storage (cache).
  • Anthropic: AI processing of email content. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial API terms.
  • Google Cloud Pub/Sub: notifications about new email.

Storage and security

The app encrypts Google access tokens before it stores them. Connections to the app use HTTPS.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Delete your data

You can disconnect the app at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete all stored data, send an email to the contact address below.
